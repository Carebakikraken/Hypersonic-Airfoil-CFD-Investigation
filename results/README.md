## Results & Findings

The CFD investigation demonstrated clear shockwave formation and pressure variation behaviour across the analysed hypersonic airfoil geometries.

Key findings from the investigation include:

* Stable solver convergence achieved across Mach 1–6 simulations
* Shockwave intensity increased significantly with higher Mach conditions
* Pressure distribution varied substantially near leading-edge regions
* Mesh independence validation improved simulation reliability
* Double-wedge geometries demonstrated strong oblique shockwave formation characteristics

The investigation successfully validated the effectiveness of CFD methodologies for analysing hypersonic aerodynamic behaviour under compressible flow conditions.
